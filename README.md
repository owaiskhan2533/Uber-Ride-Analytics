# 🚖 Uber Ride Analytics Dashboard

An interactive and comprehensive Power BI business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze global Uber operations, key business metrics, revenue trends, ride volumes, and customer behavior[cite: 3].

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project processes and visualizes ride data to deliver actionable business insights[cite: 3]. It explores various critical dimensions including vehicle performance, payment methods, customer segmentation, ride completion rates, and cancellation reasons.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Insights
* **Operational Overview:** Tracks total booking counts, completed rides, total distance covered, total revenue generated, and lost bookings.
* **Vehicle Performance Analysis:** Compares metrics across different vehicle types (Auto, Bike, Go Mini, Go Sedan, Premier Sedan, Uber XL).
* **Financial & Payment Insights:** Analyzes revenue distribution across multiple payment channels including UPI, Cash, Uber Wallet, Credit Card, and Debit Card.
* **Cancellation & Incomplete Ride Metrics:** Breaks down cancellation causes and identifies key operational bottlenecks.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzes top customers by revenue, trip frequency, and ride distribution.
